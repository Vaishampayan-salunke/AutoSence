| **DEC-001** | 2026-11-14 | Primary workspace selection: Google Antigravity | Solo research & development unified environment for pipeline orchestration, model training, FastAPI backend, and React dashboard. | VS Code, PyCharm, pure CLI | All development, scripts, and logs are centralized within the V:\BDT workspace. |
